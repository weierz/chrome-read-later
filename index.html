Take a look at the code ↗
Is Read Later really free?

Yes. Read Later is free and open source, licensed under AGPL-3.0. You can explore the code, report an issue, or contribute on GitHub.

Do I need to create an account?

No separate Read Later account is needed. To sync your reading list between computers, use the same Google account with Chrome Sync enabled.

What happens to my reading data?

Saved links, titles, and reading positions use Chrome storage. Synced entries use Chrome Sync; your archive and overflow items stay on this computer. Read Later may fetch a saved page to get its title and request site icons from Google, sharing the website’s domain. Read the privacy policy.

Does it remember every page and video?

Reading progress works on most web pages and can vary with the window size and page layout. Video progress supports the first HTML video on a page. PDFs and some sites are not supported.

What if my Chrome Sync storage is full?

With local overflow enabled, new items stay on this computer and appear in your reading list. They retry syncing when space becomes available. Local overflow items won’t appear on your other computers until they sync.
